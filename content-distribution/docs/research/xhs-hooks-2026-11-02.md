## Synthesis: a story is not an automation template

- **Source hook:** actual strong dialogue/face, character vulnerability, or an unexpected response. Don't invent secret identities or supernatural twists.
- **Narrative arc:** social pressure → actual character choice → visible consequence or real unfinished question. This is our *proposed* test structure, not a proven formula nor an accurately quoted “three-event hook” taxonomy.
- **Observer POV:** informal outside commentary supported by images: e.g. “Everyone waited for a meltdown; he walked away.” Never label a viewer observation as character dialogue or claim it is a first-person character confession.
- **Tool:** choose shots and story first, then execute with Markcut Markdown, preserving subtitle readability, source audio, watermarks and correct link placement.
- **Legal gate:** RS Boost permits specified Advertising Materials within a promotional context and limits misleading edit, unrelated dubbing/narration and removed marks. Don't simply use creator advice to rewrite original dialogue or add a new voice.
- **Measurement:** only actual TikTok/YouTube exposed watch metrics, comments, app link clicks, attributed qualifying purchases and settled CPS can differentiate strategies. Missing metrics are unverified, not zero.
