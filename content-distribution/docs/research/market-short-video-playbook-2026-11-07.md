# Source-based content distribution hypotheses — 2026-11-07

This is a reproducible editorial research reference, **not** evidence of actual campaign success or authorized use of provider clips. Generated experimental reels must be evaluated using real observation and referral receipts.

## Evidence-backed practices

- [TikTok Creative Best Practices](https://ads.tiktok.com/resources/help/article/creative-best-practices) and [TikTok creative tips](https://ads.tiktok.com/business/en/blog/creative-best-practices-top-performing-ads): 9:16 native framing, early visual/voice hook, concise narrative body and relevant CTA. Specific timing is an experiment, not a deterministic algorithm.
- [YouTube Shorts discovery](https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/11914225?hl=en): Shorts performance depends on viewer behavior, retention and satisfaction. There is no assured minimum frequency to force reach. Write a clearer opening and complete micro-arc for each variant.
- [Meta original creator guidance, 2026](https://about.fb.com/news/2026/03/rewarding-original-creators-on-facebook/): low-value reposting is not the same as meaningful creative transformation. Original editing and lawful licenses are distinct gates.
- [FTC disclosure guidance](https://www.ftc.gov/business-guidance/resources/disclosures-101-social-media-influencers): material connections require noticeable disclosure to the audience; a relevant video disclosure should appear in the video itself, not only a buried caption.
- [RS Boost official Content Hub](https://cps.reelshort.com/resource-square) and [ReelShort agreement](https://cps.reelshort.com/user/agreement): authorized media, episode-specific link and original audio are central; do not upload provider originals to cloud AI systems or dub/commentate without verifying the relevant grants.
- Existing first-hand small-sample [XHS creator observations](xhs-hooks-2026-11-02.md) suggest conversational POV and intrigue but have **no measured conversion attribution**. Avoid claiming viral formulas.

## Market test design

1. Source truth: Vision evidence must name identifiable action, quote, reaction, time range and its uncertainty; reject fabricated twists or an invented speaker map.
2. Strategist creates >=3 **materially different** ideas per eligible source: quote-first confrontation (English TikTok), self-respect/empathy mini-story (English Shorts), grounded viewer-as-commentator (Chinese XHS). Unsupported destination/account or editing permission is HOLD.
3. Each idea has a unique first 1–2s source-supported conflict, clear story beat and truthful watch-next destination; copy/caption changes alone are not another hypothesis. Mark attribution disclosures in the relevant language.
4. Editor translates approved idea inventory into Markcut `video.md` with scene source ranges, renders and verifies real audio/video and provenance. Operator only publishes when campaign account/grant/actual media have been verified.
5. Analyst uses same exposure windows and records impressions, 2s/6s holds, completion, clicks, attributed purchases, commissions, actual payout **as separate optional observations**. Missing numbers remain null, not zero. A tiny test is exploratory, not statistically proven.

This project first passes a fully local private synthetic fixture to prove the shortcut runner; success on synthetic footage is not a commercial release or evidence of an RS Boost creative winner.
