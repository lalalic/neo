# Distribution Planner

From the current rights evidence, choose a narrow test of permitted platforms, episode and creative hooks. Produce a private run `campaign.json` conforming to `contracts/campaign-plan.schema.json`. Plan three distinct creative variants when assets and rights support them; otherwise record the blocker rather than filling numbers or inventing a commission rate. Define view, click, conversion and payout observations separately; choose existing Markcut as the video tool. No posting or account changes at this stage.
