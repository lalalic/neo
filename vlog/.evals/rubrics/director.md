# Director creative MD reviewer — six independent axes (0..5)

Judge **story.md + personal-story.md + visual-brief.md**, cross-check source manifest and Visioner summary; never grade JSON presence as story quality.

- `whole_batch_judgment`: Does the Director meaningfully evaluate ALL provided footage, integrate useful context/cutaways/ambient details, and justify exclusions? Using every shot blindly is NOT the objective. A single-clip cut from 6 relevant clips is usually weak unless strongly justified.
- `complete_lived_story`: Does it deliver an understandable beginning, lived process and specific human ending/open question? Multiple beats across time, not a neat but skeletal three-line outline. No fixed length, conflict or location quota.
- `creator_personality`: Does it offer believable FIRST-PERSON reflections, gently mischievous humor, irony, teasing or observational commentary tied to what was filmed? Imaginative expression is encouraged; invented events, dialogue or personal emotional testimony stated as fact are not.
- `audience_resonance`: Which of AI, IT, work permit/PR, parenting, travel, midlife does it focus on? Does a viewer in China have a relatable question or observation, without empty scenery or preachy motivation?
- `visual_story_design`: Do planned visual variations, action continuity, meaningful reaction shots, inserts, source speech and sound shape the story, rather than only describing what is on camera?
- `truth_and_provenance`: Are source-linked observable events separated from inference and creative banter? Does it avoid made-up private history, claims about food safety, locations/routes, or unverified ASR quotes?

For each axis, score 0=absent/misleading, 1=very weak, 2=limited, 3=workable but generic, 4=distinctive and executable, 5=exceptional and grounded. Quote specific content, not vague encouragement. Penalize unexplained omissions, not low raw duration. Request two radically different creative treatments where helpful and preserve documentary truth.
