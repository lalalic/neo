# Editor creative MD reviewer — six independent axes (0..5)

Judge the **executable Markcut video.md** plus EDL/source evidence, not descriptions of hypothetical effects. An instruction saying “cinematic zoom” with no rendered effect receives no credit for executable motion.

- `material_orchestration`: Does Editor consider the full batch and use relevant inserts, cutaways, reactions, callbacks, establishing/mood frames? A narrow one-source sequence needs a specific artistic reason; maximizing shot count blindly is bad.
- `visual_craft`: Executable motivated motion/effects: Markcut native effects and transitions, ImageMotion/2.5D, shotcraft recipe, React/JSX overlays or title graphics when warranted. No credit for fixed monochrome slideshow or just writing “apply transition” in instruction. More transitions is not always better.
- `sound_personality`: Source speech, environment, carefully timed BGM, short comic stingers and recognizable “给生活加点料” brand motif where appropriate; no accidental complete muting or music-only repetition. Audio quality remains unverified until genuinely listened to.
- `narrative_pacing`: A story with progression and playful/quiet breathing room, purposeful rhythm and varied visual distance. Not merely one original clip after another or repetitive fades. No duration/beat quota.
- `meaningful_creativity`: Imaginative non-factual reaction captions, split screens, thought cards, match cuts and observational irony that serve the real event, without fabricating footage, quotes, maps, facts or risky claims.
- `source_fidelity_and_execution`: Source IDs/hashes/timecodes preserved and compatible with EDL, no fake action, effects and components genuinely executable in current Markcut. Do not claim actual render, listening or playback PASS from Markdown alone.

Score each 0..5 with specific source ID/scene evidence, executable improvement, and honest unseen-media uncertainty. Scores are advisory, not a publication gate. One artistically deliberate straight-cut documentary can score well with a compelling reason. Recommend a concrete alternate cut with shotcraft or a JSX component when the film has become a plain footage stack.
