# Story familiarity review (optional editorial strategy)

Source: creator note *强烈建议普通人不要拍日常vlog* (XHS note `6acf75be000000001802a3e3`), research transcript captured 2026-10-23. Automatic speech recognition is noisy; these are interpreted ideas, not verified verbatim quotes or algorithm promises.

The editor may apply **Real Situation → Inner Thoughts → Relatable Expression → Audience Takeaway** to a story while **separating authentic source observations from intentionally fictionalized first-person storytelling** (see [Personal Story-first](personal-story-first.md)). A routine event need not be made artificially dramatic. The strategy is optional; `not_applicable` is a valid reasoned outcome, not a failing story.

## Five required editorial review questions (when strategy is used)

| Check | What to verify | Fail/revise boundary |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Authentic situation | A real visual anchor is sourced; everyday fictionalized setbacks are separately labeled as creative details | Never invent failures as recorded facts or owner testimony; fictional embarrassment must be labeled as creative |
| Inner thoughts | Actual thoughts require recorded speech, notes or owner confirmation; proposed fictionalized internal monologue is creative writing | Do not infer private thoughts as fact from images; explicitly tag fictional monologue |
| Familiar voice | Candid, human, appropriately light or self-aware wording reflects the person's approved voice; not generic motivational narration | Do not force self-deprecation, sensitive disclosure or claimed inner monologue |
| Audience takeaway | The episode offers a truthful observation, relatable question, useful idea or emotional payoff, without a manufactured lesson | No unsupported advice or false implication of universal experience |
| Information density | Each kept beat provides story progress, character insight, context, emotional turn or payoff; remove redundant chronology while respecting context | Do not edit out context to misrepresent source or shorten below a coherent story |

For `story.md`, include an `Audience familiarity review` section recording each check as `PASS`, `REVISE`, or `NOT_APPLICABLE`, a one-sentence rationale and source/owner-confirmation pointer. Do not report factual authenticity PASS merely because a fictional story sounds plausible. For fictionalized inner monologue, assess voice and narrative coherence separately from proof of the creator's actual thoughts.

For `visual-brief.md`, attribute actual recorded quotations to evidence; mark invented monologue `fictionalized voiceover`, not verified source speech, and retain meaningful original audio and quiet moments. Video Director and Market reviewers should inspect the same five checks against the immutable preview version; their existing production/review gates remain unchanged.

Synthetic example (**not a real event**): a cyclist attempts a hill again after a prior incomplete ride; the editor may frame an authentic second attempt as the hook **only if** the previous attempt, current footage and cyclist intention are verified; otherwise label the premise `fictionalized personal story` and do not claim real recorded attempts or show fake footage.
