# Story familiarity review (optional editorial strategy)

Source: creator note *强烈建议普通人不要拍日常vlog* (XHS note `6acf75be000000001802a3e3`), research transcript captured 2026-10-23. Automatic speech recognition is noisy; these are interpreted ideas, not verified verbatim quotes or algorithm promises.

The editor may apply **Real Situation → Inner Thoughts → Relatable Expression → Audience Takeaway** to a story **only when each statement has authentic evidence or explicit owner confirmation**. A routine event need not be made artificially dramatic. The strategy is optional; `not_applicable` is a valid reasoned outcome, not a failing story.

## Five required editorial review questions (when strategy is used)

| Check | What to verify | Fail/revise boundary |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Authentic situation | A real, specific situation, friction or surprise is grounded in a source media ID and range, or owner-confirmed notes | Never invent failures, embarrassment, conflict or awkward moments |
| Inner thoughts | The person's actual motivation, doubt or change in thinking comes from recorded speech, contemporaneous notes or owner confirmation | Do not infer private thoughts, feelings or backstory from images |
| Familiar voice | Candid, human, appropriately light or self-aware wording reflects the person's approved voice; not generic motivational narration | Do not force self-deprecation, sensitive disclosure or claimed inner monologue |
| Audience takeaway | The episode offers a truthful observation, relatable question, useful idea or emotional payoff, without a manufactured lesson | No unsupported advice or false implication of universal experience |
| Information density | Each kept beat provides story progress, character insight, context, emotional turn or payoff; remove redundant chronology while respecting context | Do not edit out context to misrepresent source or shorten below a coherent story |

For `story.md`, include an `Audience familiarity review` section recording each check as `PASS`, `REVISE`, or `NOT_APPLICABLE`, a one-sentence rationale and source/owner-confirmation pointer. Never report `PASS` merely because a synthetic narration sounds plausible. With no reliable internal thoughts, mark that check `NOT_APPLICABLE` and retain a truthful observational story.

For `visual-brief.md`, tie every quoted line and introspective beat to its source evidence, classify suggested lines as `editorial suggestion / approval pending`, and retain meaningful original audio and quiet moments. Video Director and Market reviewers should inspect the same five checks against the immutable preview version; their existing production/review gates remain unchanged.

Synthetic example (**not a real event**): a cyclist attempts a hill again after a prior incomplete ride; the editor may frame an authentic second attempt as the hook **only if** the previous attempt, current footage and the cyclist's stated intention are in source evidence. Otherwise, keep it as a fictional test fixture and never present it as an actual episode.
