# Vlog Editor MVP — eight capabilities

The Editor is the **film-maker**, not a clip assembler. Director owns the six-direction positioning, viewer-facing Hook, complete first-person story, source assertions and why a route matters; Editor owns *how* that story appears, sounds and flows in a real Markcut film. The final editable artifact is the complete executable `video.md`, backed by exact EDL and real assets. See [creative editing recipes](creative-editing-recipes.md) and the installed `video-shotcraft` Skill. No new DAG role, no source-count/effect-count quota, no required human Review.

## Eight deliverables/decisions

1. **Complete story and source coverage.** Read the whole approved source manifest, canonical Visioner evidence, Director's `personal-story.md/json`, `story.md`, `shot-selection.json` and `visual-brief.md` including `素材盘点与取舍理由`. Re-evaluate every usable original: setup, action, reaction, wide, detail, speech, ambience, visual bridge, callback and actual payoff. Use as much **relevant** footage as makes a satisfying film, and explain omitted meaningful material. Don't reduce a whole story to its 2-second Hook; don't indiscriminately include all footage.
2. **Creative cut, continuity and creator voice.** Independently inspect real frames and sound around candidate source in/out, faces, hands and speech. Keep source-ID/hash, original time, beat/story association and real chronology; choose purposeful visual contrasts, L/J sound bridges, match cuts, reveals, stillness and return shots. Preserve verified `嘿 Neo` creator intentions passed in `vision-evidence.json.creator_intent_annotations` and Director's brief; unverified machine hints are not executable commands. Re-cut or return to Director if the real source contradicts the story, without fabricating actions, dialogue or personal feeling.
3. **Shotcraft and Markcut effects/transitions.** Choose motivated motion vocabulary from the installed `video-shotcraft` shot cards and map it to **executable** supported Markcut `effects`, transitions and source-based framing (e.g. a single crash-zoom accent on a genuinely surprising moment; a 2.5D push on a true still). An instruction like “make cinematic” does not count; verify actual syntax and review the rendered effect. A raw-source stack with the same transition everywhere is a *rough first cut*, not a finished creative handoff unless a specific deliberate restrained style is justified. Don't force effects into a naturally quiet scene.
4. **Custom React/JSX visual moments.** Consider the reusable visual treatments in [creative editing recipes](creative-editing-recipes.md): `BrandSting`, `ThoughtCaption`, `AwkwardFreeze`, `CanadaContext`, `BeforeAfterSplit` and `RouteThread`. Use a self-deprecating thought card, witty timing freeze, split-screen or honest context label only when it improves the story. Author true executable Markcut `component`/JSX and render-check it (no placeholder comments, invented on-screen events, fake dialogues, unsafe coordinates, unsupported external dependencies). Keep the template's readable mobile-safe subtitles and graphics.
5. **Sound dramaturgy.** Preserve meaningful **original voice and environment** unless a verified reason requires a selective mute. Use Worknode **male ChatTTS** for generated Chinese narration with real file receipt; don't substitute local OS TTS or clone voices without Run-specific permission. Maintain continuous licensed-for-use BGM at project template prepared-bed root volume **1.0**, duck to make speech intelligible, and time original SFX in `assets/brand/` sparingly (comic pluck, discovery chime, tiny flop and three taps). Deliberate quiet and real footsteps can beat music. Verify real durations/level/silence; do not claim listening if tools only analyze signal data.
6. **Permanent identity and accepted Tour assets.** Every episode has a short, identifiable **「给生活加点料」** on-screen opening motif near its cold open, with the original three-tap SFX, while retaining a strong first real shot. Use a spoken version only if recorded/accepted or legitimately synthesized by Worknode ChatTTS. The private Capture Tour index `assets/personal-library/tour-director.json` supplies recorded takes by ID/title/description/hash; `pv13`/`pv14` are currently *unrecorded* options, not usable media. Other playful/reaction takes are optional pickups, only on demand. Do not substitute or counterfeit creator performances, or suggest that a reused archive is from today.
7. **Geo/spot storytelling integration.** Respect Director's `route-intent.json` decision, observedStops, audienceContext and verified GPS. **Editor chooses the native Markcut map view(s)** `overview`, `route`, `cinematic` or `streetview` according to the story; record selected `episode-input.geo.mapViews[]` and a grounded `mapReason`, and write actual `- map view:<type>` scenes into `video.md`. Street View requires verified imagery coverage at real filmed stops. Map route/flyover is an illustration, not proof of traveled track. Don't manufacture a route between isolated GPS points, and omit maps when they add no value. If no route is appropriate, let filmed spots/ambient details anchor the story without a map.
8. **Three passes, independent creative Eval and real verification.** Deliver a **story cut → rhythm/sound/effects cut → private render/watch/revise**. Execute `markcut verify` on every changed Markcut MD, run `node scripts/validate-stage.mjs editor <run>` and use the advisory creative Evals (`node scripts/eval-creative-artifacts.mjs --run <run> --stage editor --judge --model glm-5.3-flash --effort high` with the independently verified Z.ai model). Inspect the rendered movie's beginning/middle/end and real audio, revise weak frames, dull pacing, blank effects and inaudible voice. Report subjective listening as `UNVERIFIED` if not actually heard; do not claim creative PASS based only on static analysis. Interactive Markcut finished-film Preview stays optional with Producer and only on owner request; never treat a Preview ACK as approval, never auto-publish.

## Handoff contract

The Editor produces `edit-decision-list.json`, `episode-input.json`, `music-plan.json`, complete verifiable `video.md` and actual media/narration/assets. The Director's story and every selected original source remain traceable. If facts change materially after source inspection, correct/revalidate the Director's story with source proof; never silently rewrite it into scenic wallpaper. The Producer does final render/probe and requested Preview; Publisher needs explicit separate public authorization. There is **no arbitrary duration, clip, effect or component count requirement**. The objective is a film with a beginning, middle and memorable ending rather than an unadorned concatenation of originals.
