# Vlog production style: one story, real route, one continuous soundtrack

The Director writes a **complete, first-person lived-experience story** before selecting scenes; see [Personal Story-first](personal-story-first.md). The arc is intention/curiosity → real exploration/action → specific discovery/outcome, with **optional** setback and contrast. Forcing a problem on a peaceful walk is a failure. The accepted `episode-input.json` includes `story.personal_story_id`, `story.goal`, `story.outcome`, `story.evidenceRef` and `story.progression[]` in narrative order with `sceneId`, `purpose` and `delivery`. Delivery is `source-speech` (verified by actual listening), `voiceover` (real text/audio asset), or `visual-story` (a purposeful bridge). Start and payoff remain audible via verified speech or a short voiceover, but do not narrate every scene: leave up to two consecutive visual-only beats with real ambience or silence when images carry the middle of the story. Unreviewed speech transcripts are not verified dialogue. Producer rejects scene-by-scene scenic commentary rather than constructing a story.

## Geographic storytelling

Use verified EXIF/QuickTime GPS for private production and explicit owner permission before displaying exact/sensitive locations publicly. **The primary audience is in China, while the creator's experience is in Canada**: when meaningful, open with a grounded city/province/region orientation in plain Chinese, briefly explain where the stops are and why they matter to this story, and add one useful local fact or contrast a Chinese viewer might not know. Research place/history/culture claims from credible sources and keep the source reference in `route-intent.json` (optional `audienceContext[]`) or `story.md`; omit unsupported facts. Never turn a simple outing into a geography lecture.

Prefer a short, narrated map/location overview at a natural travel/scene transition (`geo.insertAfterSceneId`), rather than always after the hook; omit a moving route if trustworthy GPS only establishes isolated stops. Record trusted stop provenance and chronology; Directions geometry is a **suggested navigation path**, not a recorded journey. Don't invent visited landmarks, modes of travel, distances or local events. An actual track requires its own evidence and supported renderer. Map and commentary should advance orientation or discovery, not interrupt the story; credible GPS missing means no invented route.

## Transitions and soundtrack

- **Pacing follows story and hearing, not clip upload order.** Start with an engaging moment, alternate observation/action with brief spoken interpretation, and give strong images or authentic ambience room to breathe. A cut, similar motion, recurring subject or audio bridge can connect different media; choose consistent direction, sound continuity and justified transitions rather than uniform slideshow timing. Avoid abrupt topic switches and unnecessary narration over every landscape shot.

- Every adjacent scene requires a continuity rationale and a supported, **actually rendered** boundary transition. For the cinematic default use a short `fade`; direct `cut` is valid for justified match/action cuts and in the explicit cuts variant. Markcut currently applies one transition type and duration per series; never silently promise mixed effects that this DSL cannot render.
- The executable [Markcut template](../templates/cinematic-vlog.md) owns the BGM selection and continuous root audio leaf. A pre-mixed continuous asset matching the **exact template song** is required for vocal/bed/ending changes; the template selection itself does not grant licensing. The root `audio isBackground:true` is a global Markcut stream and loops to the full episode duration. **Avoid double ducking:** from 2026-11-09 forward, prepare a single mixed bed with Neo's project-agnostic `scripts/audio-bed.mjs`, and set its root BGM playback volume to `1.0` in the canonical template. Root `1.0` is a playback multiplier for already-balanced audio, not automatic loudness normalization; older Run revisions keep their historical `0.7` contract. Do not duck a prepared bed again. Validate voice/music balance on the final audio stream. Preserve narrator intelligibility and verify actual output levels.
  The old mean-dBFS threshold is not a valid judgment of human-perceived music/voice balance;
  use Markcut verification for the resource/DSL, and evaluate the final mixed
  audio when a listening review is requested. Use an authorized seamless loop / crossfade
  source where needed; Producer verifies the rendered output technically.
- Maintain natural creator narration and meaningful original ambience above the music. Duck BGM under speech and source sound using a verified pre-mixed track when the current DSL can't sidechain automatically. The same root BGM continues through route clips, stills and video. Route narration is foreground; it is not a new theme song. One-off SFX accents may be layered; a second BGM requires an evidence-based major story/emotional change approved by Director.
- No forced multi-song montage, blanket dissolve on every match cut, abrupt silence at scene boundaries or unrelated 20-30-second GPS insertion.

## Noninteractive Editor verification

Editor verifies each authored Markcut `.md`, scene order, real source
intervals, declared foreground narration, continuous BGM and route references.
It does not need a native viewer or to listen/watch the output. Producer renders
and verifies real video/audio streams. Optional human Preview remains on demand,
and cannot be implied by Markcut parser or render success.

## Story-paced media coverage (source evidence vs screen time)

- **Narrative beats set the intended timeline time.** A source clip `sourceInSeconds..sourceOutSeconds` is only the original media interval. Its `sourceDuration = sourceOutSeconds - sourceInSeconds` is not a cap on the presented `timelineDuration`. `playbackSpeed = sourceDuration / timelineDuration` for editorial re-timing (for example `1.55 / 3.10 = 0.5`). All lengths must be explicit and coherent in Editor EDL and Producer scene input. Use Markcut's native `startFrom`/`endAt` trimming and `playbackRate` for supported slow motion (the current video renderer caps rates above 1); do not introduce a Vlog-owned media-treatment pipeline. Keep the original source path/hash and intended timeline duration in the Editor EDL. Do not slow source dialogue as if it were authentic spoken speech; mute slowed audio, verify and layer original ambience separately when required.
- **Still photos are usable moving coverage.** Assign a deliberate `override.motion`: `slowPush`, `zoomOut`, `driftLeft`, `driftRight`, or `none`. Different crops/angles and motivated callbacks can show the same verified source more than once; don't depict a repeated moment as a newly recorded event. Media coverage must vary according to the narrative turn, not randomly.
- **Longer is not automatically better.** Keep the complete intention, lived development and outcome (with difficulty only when real), and eliminate empty scenic holds. One long photo, synthetic black filler, or instruction-only Markcut nodes do not satisfy a beat. Review actual motion and readable captions in the final MP4.
