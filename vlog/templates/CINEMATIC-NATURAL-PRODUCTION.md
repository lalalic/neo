# Cinematic Natural — editorial acceptance guide

This guide is **not an additional template**. All executable Markcut layout, opening/body/payoff scene blocks, theme-song selection, root BGM, captions, source and voice volume, still motion and transition style live in [cinematic-vlog.md](cinematic-vlog.md). Its route subvideo templates are [geo-story.md](geo-story.md) and [route-overview.md](route-overview.md).

Director chooses a source-grounded goal and payoff; Editor selects the permitted source cuts and justified transitions. The Producer binds those decisions into the Markdown template. Personal footage remains the factual authority. A map may establish geographic context, but computed navigation is not evidence of a traveled path; a street view or animated aerial is a reference, not creator footage.

For geographic narration, use source-linked visual evidence and reliable capture times. Generate actual audio, never only text. Markcut map and narration must be parallel siblings. Real route output must contain visible tiles, supported marker order, intelligible narration and any relevant photo/context cut. When maps fail, do not claim an exported MP4 proves they were visible in Preview.

Use one continuous authorized BGM asset according to the selected Markdown template. Never substitute unlicensed music, invent rights, restart the song at each cut or drop BGM when Browser TTS plays. Source environment sound and intelligible narration should remain audible.

The [Stage contract](../docs/episode-stage-contract.md) owns source authorization, revisions, human approval and Final QA. [Geo story evidence](../docs/geo-story-production.md) owns factual route constraints. Change executable creative structure in the Markcut templates, not here.
